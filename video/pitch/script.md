## English voiceover — 90 seconds

### 00:00–00:10 · Hook

> Moving to an unfamiliar city looks like a housing search. But what you repeat every day isn't a listing. It's an ordinary day.

### 00:10–00:22 · Problem

> Most tools ask about rent, commute, and bedrooms. They miss what makes daily life work: if work ends at seven thirty, can you still eat, unwind, meet a friend, or reach a park?

### 00:22–00:36 · Agent loop

> City Afterimage is a relocation agent. Users set work, commute, and who and what they want nearby, then choose between real-life scenes. Before showing a place name, it turns those choices into a lifestyle profile.

### 00:36–00:52 · Recognition before recommendation

> That order matters. First, users check whether the profile feels true, and can remove or change anything that doesn't. Then the agent connects it to Wuhan coordinates, routes, nearby facilities, and twenty-two curated neighborhood clusters.

### 00:52–01:09 · Evidence and three decisions

> It returns three choices: best overall fit, least daily friction, and a feasible step toward the life they want. Each includes an evidence passport with sources, a clear trade-off, and a simulated weekday and weekend.

### 01:09–01:22 · Replan

> The user can push back. Tighten the commute limit from forty to thirty-five minutes, or reject one piece of evidence. The recommendation, its evidence, and the simulated day all update together.

### 01:22–01:30 · OPC close

> For one mover, City Afterimage combines the work of a local guide, relocation agent, and data analyst. It never claims one perfect home.

## 中文演练稿 — 90 秒

### 00:00–00:10 · 开场

> 搬去一座陌生城市，看起来是在找房。但你每天重复的不是一条房源，而是一个普通日常。

### 00:10–00:22 · 问题

> 大多数工具只问租金、通勤和户型，却漏掉了决定日常能否成立的问题：七点半下班后，你还能吃饭、放松、见朋友，或走进公园吗？

### 00:22–00:36 · Agent 闭环

> 城市潜像是一款迁居决策 Agent。用户设定工作、通勤，以及想靠近的人与地点，再在具体生活场景中做选择。地名出现前，Agent 会把这些选择转成生活画像。

### 00:36–00:52 · 先认出，再推荐

> 这个顺序很重要。用户先判断画像像不像自己，也能删改不准确的内容。确认后，Agent 才把画像连接到武汉坐标、路线、周边设施和二十二个策展生活圈。

### 00:52–01:09 · 证据与三种决策

> 系统给出三种选择：综合最合拍、日常最省力，以及现实可行又更接近向往生活的地方。每个选择都有标注来源的证据护照、明确妥协，以及模拟的工作日和周末。

### 01:09–01:22 · 接受反驳

> 用户可以反驳：把通勤上限从四十分钟收紧到三十五分钟，或否定一条证据。推荐、证据和模拟的一天会同步更新。

### 01:22–01:30 · OPC 收束

> 城市潜像为一个异地入职者组合了本地向导、迁居顾问和数据分析师的能力，但它从不声称存在唯一完美的住处。

## On-screen claim boundaries

Say:

- evidence-backed, explainable decision;
- real coordinates and provider-labelled route/facility evidence;
- three decision roles with explicit trade-offs;
- 22 curated Wuhan living-circle seeds;
- replan when the user disagrees.

Do not say:

- scientifically proven perfect neighborhood;
- real-time data from every platform;
- guaranteed commute, safety, rent, availability, or satisfaction;
- live Dianping, Meituan, or Xiaohongshu integration.
